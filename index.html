About S&H Rentals Co

Events, equipment & transport — all under one roof.

S&H Rentals Co is a proudly Sowetan event services business. We supply event equipment rentals, professional event planning and reliable transportation for weddings, parties, corporate functions, funerals, church gatherings and community events across Soweto and greater Johannesburg.

From a single tent to a full wedding for 500 guests, we handle the equipment, the décor, the setup and the shuttle logistics — so you can focus on your guests.

  • Full equipment rental: tents, chairs, tables, stage, sound, lighting
  • Event planning, décor styling and on-site coordination
  • Guest transport: shuttle vans, minibuses and luxury sedans
  • Delivery, setup, collection and after-event cleanup
